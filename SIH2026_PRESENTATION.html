Idea & Proposed Solution

Grassroots Rural Challenges Directly Bridged to 44 Higher Educational Institutions

Core Innovation
The Current Disconnected Reality
Real-World Analogy: When a community handpump breaks or arsenic contaminates water in Baghmara Block, a villager must travel 30 km to lodge a physical paper grievance. The complaint enters a bureaucratic black hole with zero tracking. Concurrently, 5 km away at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, senior engineering students simulate generic textbook problems for their final semester project.
  • High friction: Literacy barriers and complex English/formal web forms prevent rural reporting.
  • Academic detachment: Students lack access to verified ground engineering problems.
The JoharSetu Bridge Model
The Bridge: JoharSetu transforms village grievances into accredited university capstones and corporate CSR co-funded solutions. Citizens speak in local dialects; regional universities diagnose and resolve the defects for academic credits.
  • Multilingual Voice & Photo Ingestion: Conversational assistant supports Hindi, Santhali, Mundari, and Ho with instant live GPS auto-capture.
  • Crowdsourced Upvote Engine: Communities upvote neighborhood challenges, driving algorithmic "Hype" ranking to prioritize critical bottlenecks.
  • Autonomous Spatial HEI Routing: Geodesic distance calculation routes tickets to the nearest specialized engineering department among 44 mapped universities.
  • Identity Anonymity Engine: Default public masking of citizen phone and identity with selective reveal for government officers.
🎓

NEP 2020 Academic Credit Integration

🤝

CSR Crowdfunding & Co-Financing Hub
